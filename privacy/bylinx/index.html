Privacy Policy
Bylinx

Effective November 18, 2026

Bylinx has no accounts and asks for no personal details. To compare news coverage, it sends the topic you tap or the term you search to our servers and to the services that find and analyze articles. It contains no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics, and we do not sell or share your information.

This policy describes how Bylinx (“the App”), published by Mind Upgrade LLC (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies only to the App.

Information we do not collect

The App has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other details about you.

Information the App uses

  • Topics and searches. When you open a topic or search for one, that topic or search term is sent to our servers so we can find and compare coverage of it. It is not linked to you or to any identifier.
  • IP address. Like any internet service, our servers see your device's IP address when the App connects. We use it only to prevent abuse by limiting how many requests can be made. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version for this, never the address itself.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe to Bylinx Pro, our subscription provider records an anonymous identifier created on your device, the plan you chose and its status, so the App knows to unlock Pro features.

Information stored on your device

The App saves the topic list, articles you save to your Library, which topics you have viewed, and whether you have seen the introduction. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you delete the App.

Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers that process information only to run the App for us. We do not sell or share your information with anyone for advertising or marketing.

The App does not send anything you write about yourself to an AI provider. What it analyzes is published news coverage, plus the topic or term you chose.

How long we keep it

  • Comparison results are cached for up to 12 hours so repeat requests are fast. Search lookups are cached until the end of that day. Neither is linked to you.
  • Rate-limit records (hashed IP only) expire after 2 days.
  • Server logs are kept for about 1 day. They record only the first few characters of a search, never the full text.
  • Information on your device stays until you delete the App.
  • Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies.

Advertising and tracking

The App contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking. It does not use your device's advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Payments

Subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store and processed by Apple. We never receive your card or payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Your choices and rights

Because the App has no accounts, we hold very little that can be tied to you. You can still ask us what we hold, or ask us to correct or delete it. See how to make a privacy request. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your local privacy law, and we will honor them.

International users

Our servers and service providers are in the United States. If you use the App from elsewhere, your information is processed in the United States, under contractual safeguards our providers offer.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
