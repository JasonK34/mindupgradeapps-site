Privacy Policy
Ladder: Retro Climb

Effective November 14, 2026

Ladder: Retro Climb collects no personal information. The game makes no network connections, contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and the only thing it saves is your best score, on your device. Nothing leaves your phone.

This policy describes how Ladder: Retro Climb (“the App”), published by Mind Upgrade LLC (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies only to the App.

Information we collect

None. The App does not ask for, collect, or receive any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no way to enter a name, email address, or any other details.

Network activity

The game itself does not connect to the internet and sends nothing off your device, to us or to anyone else. The only exception is the Privacy Policy link, which opens this page in your browser when you tap it.

Storage on your device

The App saves one thing: your best score, so it is still there the next time you play. It is stored only on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you delete the App.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The App contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits that collect data. We do not sell or share information, because we have none.

Device permissions

The App does not request access to your location, camera, photos, microphone, contacts, or any other permission.

Purchases

The App is free and has no in-app purchases.

Apple

Apple may collect information when you download or use apps through the App Store, under its own privacy policy. If you have opted in to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We do not receive anything that identifies you.

Children

Because the App collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the App ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
