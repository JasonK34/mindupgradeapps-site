Privacy Requests

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us not to sell or share it. We respond to every request, free of charge.

Email a privacy request

jason@mindupgradeapps.com

What to include

  • Which app your request is about.
  • What you would like us to do: access, correct, or delete your information, or opt out of sale or sharing.
  • Anything that helps us find your information, such as roughly when you used the app and on what kind of device.

Our apps do not use accounts, so we never ask for a password. We may ask a follow-up question to confirm a request is genuine before acting on it.

What we may hold

Stoicize: Mindset Shift. We do not store the text or voice you submit for reframing. Usage logs, which contain a random identifier generated by the app, are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. Subscription and payment records are held by Apple or Google; you can view and manage them in your App Store or Google Play account.

Ladder: Retro Climb. The app collects nothing and sends nothing to us. Your best score is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Response time

We aim to respond within 30 days, and always within the time your local privacy law requires.

Authorized agents

Someone acting on your behalf can submit a request. We may ask for proof that they are authorized.
