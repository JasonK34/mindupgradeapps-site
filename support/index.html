Subscriptions and billing

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so they can be managed only from your store account:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For refunds, Apple and Google decide; you can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com or from your Google Play order history.

New phone, or a subscription not showing up? Open the app's paywall or account screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.
